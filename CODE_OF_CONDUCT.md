# Code of conduct

Papers is for anyone trying to learn from research, whatever their field, level or first language. We want working
on it to feel the same way.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are new to a field, to programming, or to English.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- Criticize ideas and code, never people. Say what would make it better.
- Accept that maintainers may say no to a change, and explain why when they do.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where it applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Write to the maintainer privately through the contact on [@hBouanane](https://github.com/hBouanane)'s GitHub
profile, or open a private report from the repository's **Security** tab if it can't wait. Reports are read and
answered, and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, code or contributions that break these rules, and
may block people who keep doing so.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
